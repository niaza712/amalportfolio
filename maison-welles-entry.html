— Featured Project — 03

Maison Welles

Role — Social Media & Graphic Design Intern
Brand — Maison Welles (New York)
Timeline — Sept 2026 – Present
See the menu on Instagram ↗

Built a mood board from the brand's rebrand direction, setting the palette (moss green, rose, warm red) and a theatrical, vintage Greenwich Village tone. I then made brand collateral from it, including a pop-up key tag, matchbooks and a storefront illustration.

Designed a new logo for Maison Welles, drawn from a vintage Christofle silver shell tray in the store. I traced the tray's scalloped edge and scrolled base into a hand-inked frame, then built it out as a system: sticker-style shell frames in baby pink and butter yellow, and a one-color version for packaging and print.

Redesigned the cafe menu within a strict brand protocol: set color palette, approved typefaces and layout rules, so it reads as part of the store rather than a separate piece.

Pitched and developed content ideas for social, including a private cinema club concept for building community around the brand. Supported visual merchandising in the store and worked on the Shopify site.
